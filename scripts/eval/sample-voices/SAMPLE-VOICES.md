# CC0 / public-domain sample voices

Galion Voice — Perpetual Open MIT License. (c) 2026 Maciej Grajczyk.

These clips are not in git. No WAV is committed. They are not Samantha
or Jarvis, and they are not film audio. Piper does not use them as a
conditioning prompt. The live CPU voice is Piper `en_US-ljspeech-medium`.

The owner directed the sources below as CC0 or public domain. A local
copy can be fetched from the URL. Do not commit the audio.
