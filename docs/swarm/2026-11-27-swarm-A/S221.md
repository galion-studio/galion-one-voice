## What was already on the branch

`74fce4dc` loads the sealed pack once, from `HostSession::boot`, and only when learn and remember are both granted and `live/learn-pack.sqlite` is already a file. An empty pack does not construct `LearnPass`, does not call `attach_learned`, and does not replace the diary the card engine opened at spawn. `on_turn` does not audit the pack. `finish` only seals the clause already held in RAM. The 128 KiB gate, the 8 s first-turn deadline, and the SIGTERM deadline were not changed.

The laptop failure quoted in the PR body was the old resident-set gate: `4148f366` grew 256 KiB after turn 300 (`rss_mark=19840`, `peak=end=20096`). That commit still called `LearnPass::open().audit()` at boot. `74fce4dc` had not been soaked.
